Accessibility

My LMS aims to support students using keyboards, screen readers, mobile devices and browser zoom. We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as our accessibility target.

Using the app

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and Escape to close dialogs. Task status uses a standard selection control. Settings includes feedback for reporting a problem or requesting an improvement.

Testing and limitations

Our October 2026 review includes automated accessibility checks and keyboard and mobile review. Automated tests cannot establish complete WCAG conformance. Screen reader combinations, third-party Google sign-in screens and every possible imported document have not all been independently tested.

Report a barrier

Open Feedback in the app or in Settings. Describe the screen, what you were trying to do, your device or assistive technology, and the problem. Avoid including passwords or private course documents. We review reported barriers alongside other issues.
